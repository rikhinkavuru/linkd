How to Play

Build the chain.

Six to ten words. Each pair forms a real compound or common phrase.

BLACK → OUT → B___

BLACKOUT, OUTBACK — both real.

Typing

Type only the missing letters, or the full word. For WH___ all of WHITE, HITE, ITE are accepted.

Strikes

Four wrong guesses ends the puzzle. Non-words don't cost a strike.

Hints

Reveal a letter — no strike. Easy 3 · Medium 2 · Hard 1.
