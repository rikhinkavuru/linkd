▸ How to Play

Build the chain.

Each puzzle is a chain of 6–10 words. Adjacent words always form a real compound word or common phrase.

BLACK → O___

First word given, plus a hint for the next. Guess it correctly and the chain continues:

BLACK → OUT → B___

BLACKOUT and OUTBACK — both real phrases.

Typing

Shown letters are free — you can type only the missing letters, or the full word. For WH___ (target WHITE), all of WHITE, HITE, and ITE are accepted.

Strikes

You get 4 wrong guesses total before the puzzle ends. Same chain for everyone.

If you type something that isn't a real English word, it doesn't cost a strike — just try again.

Hints

Stuck? Hit the ▸ Hint button under the chain to reveal one more letter — no strike. Limited per puzzle:

Easy: 3 hints · Medium: 2 hints · Hard: 1 hint.

Difficulty

Easy: First two letters + word length. Strikes and hints reveal more.

Medium: First letter + word length. Strikes and hints reveal more.

Hard: First letter only — no length shown.

Your chosen difficulty is part of your share string — friends will know if you took the harder path.

Compare with Friends

Share your result. Friends do the same. Paste theirs into your Friends page to see leaderboards by mode and overall.
